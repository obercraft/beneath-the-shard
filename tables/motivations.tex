\rowcolor{white}\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Category 1: Debt, Crime \& Desperation}} \\
\midrule
01 -- 05 & The Guild's Ransom & Your party owes a crushing financial debt to a cutthroat Mining Guild. They hold your loved ones hostage on the surface until you bring back a specific weight of pure Aether-Glass. \\
06–10 & Death Row Fugitives & You were scheduled for execution at dawn in a surface kingdom. You broke out of the dungeons and ran into the only place the royal guards fear to follow: a Shard-Gate. \\
11–15 & The Stolen Cargo & While working as caravan guards, you lost a priceless shipment of refined crystal dust to bandits. If you don't replace it by harvesting raw crystals from the deep, your employers will hunt you down. \\
16–20 & Blackmarket Deal & You tried to sell illegal Shard-relics on the surface, but the buyers betrayed you. Now you are broke, wounded, and forced to dive deeper into the dark to find something valuable. \\
21–25 & Forced Conscription & The local monarch has drafted your party as "expendable scouts." You have been pushed into the Shard-Gate at spearpoint with orders to clear the first level—or never return. \\

\midrule
% --- CATEGORY 2 ---
\rowcolor{white}\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{Category 2: The Creeping Corruption}} \\
\midrule
26–30 & The Shard-Marked & One or more of your companions accidentally touched a raw vein. A glowing, crystalline pattern is slowly spreading across their skin. You are searching for the cure. \\
31–35 & Haunted by Whispers & After a minor cavern collapse, all three of you started hearing the same rhythmic, beautiful voice whispering in your minds. It calls you from the absolute bottom. \\
36–40 & Alchemical Addiction & Your characters are rogue scholars who became heavily addicted to the reality-bending high of Shard-Dust. You are running out of supply, and the only way to feed your hunger is at the source. \\
41–45 & Blood of Aethelgard & A strange ritual revealed that your party carries bloodlines tracing back to the archmages of the destroyed planet. The Shard’s magic reacts to you, and you need to find out why. \\
46–50 & The Mutated Village & Your hometown’s water supply was poisoned by a subterranean breach. The villagers are mutating into horrific beasts. You volunteered to descend and collapse the specific tunnel. \\

\midrule
51–55 & Disgraced Vanguard & You were knights who failed to protect a high-ranking lord from a Shard-beast raid. Stripped of your titles, you swore an oath of exile until you bring back the beast's head. \\
56–60 & Hunting the Star-Cult & A fanatical cult that worships the Asteroid as a cosmic god slaughtered your temple or monastery. You tracked the killers into the deep caves to exact bloody vengeance. \\
61–65 & Retrieval of Fallen & A famous group of veterans went missing in this sector a week ago. You have been hired to recover their specialized gear, their maps, or at least their bones. \\
66–70 & The Broken Seal & Your family has guarded a sealed iron vault for generations. Last night, the growing crystal veins underneath shattered the floor, and something terrifying escaped. \\
71–75 & Mercenary Contract & Purely business.
 A wealthy merchant prince is paying you a massive 
fortune in gold upfront to clear a specific subterranean path so his miners can move in safely. \\

\midrule

81–85 & Gold Rush Fever & The surface world is dying, poor, and boring. Down here, even a single handful of Aether-Glass can buy you a noble title on the surface. You get rich or die trying. \\
86–90 & Celestial Alignment & An ancient prophecy dictates that when the stars align tonight, a specific Shard-Gate will open a path to a weapon capable of overthrowing the corrupt emperor of Terramyr. \\
91–95 & The Escaped Subject & You are escorting a strange, mute child found near a crystal vein who possesses the ability to control the growth of the Shard. The factions want them dead or captured. \\
96–100 & The Prismatic Choice & In your dreams, an entity from Aethelgard showed you two futures: one where Terramyr explodes, and one where your party stands at the planet's core, holding the key to salvation. \\
