\section{Classes}

After you choose an archetype, pick one \kw{class} from that archetype's list below.
Your class determines which special actions you may learn as you grow stronger.

When the rules or adventure say you \kw{level up}, advance to the next level (1 through 10).
At the new level, \textbf{choose one} of the five special actions listed for that level and add it to your character.
You do not gain the other four; they remain unavailable unless a later rule says otherwise.

Class special actions are \textbf{in addition to} your Basic Actions and your archetype actions.
They use the same difficulty symbols as other combat actions (\easy, \medium{} \energy{1}, \hard{} \hard{}).
