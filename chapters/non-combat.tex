\section{Non-Combat Encounters}

Between delves and battles, Terramyr still demands a price. Use these five journey mechanics to frame downtime, travel, and Gate crossings.
When the adventure calls for a non-combat beat---or when a Journey Clock tick, camp, or Gate demands it---roll \D{}{100} and open the matching entry in the companion volume \textbf{Beneath The Shard --- Journey Encounters} (\texttt{encounters.pdf}).

\subsection{Journey Clock}
Track travel as a shared clock (boxes equal to the route's length). When the clock advances, pressure rises: weather, detours, and exhaustion. Filling the clock means you arrive Late, Lost, or Exhausted (GM picks one: start next fight with $-1$ party die, or roll an extra encounter).

\subsection{Resonance Stress}
Handling Aether-Glass fills a party or personal Stress track. High Stress twists body and mind. Reduce Stress mainly through Campfire Counsel, destroying crystal, or paying rare costs listed in encounters.

\subsection{Debt \& Favors}
Marks track what guilds, crowns, cults, and killers owe you---or what you owe them. Spend Favor for aid; ignore Debt and collectors write the next chapter in blood.

\subsection{Shard-Gate Omens}
Before each Gate, roll or draw an omen. Omens twist the coming delve's rules (zones, Guards, who is Marked). Heeding an omen often costs Stress or gear; ignoring it invites worse timing in combat.

\subsection{Campfire Counsel}
Between fights, assign party dice to downtime actions instead of combat: Heal, Repair, Scout, Bargain, and Lore. Success softens clocks, Stress, Debt, and Omens; failure invites the night in.
