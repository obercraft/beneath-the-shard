\section{Playing The Game}

To start a new game of \game{} you need:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dice:} at least 12 six-sided dice, and two ten-sided dice of different color.
    \item \textbf{Party Sheet:} \todo{}
    \item \textbf{World Map:} \todo{}
    \item \textbf{Scrap Paper:} \todo{}
\end{itemize}

First you create a party, then you get on a journey through \world{}. On your your journey you will
face combat- and non-combat encounters.

In combat, roll your party's shared \kw{Dice Pool} at the start of each round and assign the results to meet these thresholds. See Combat for the full procedure.

Between fights, travel and Gate crossings use non-combat beats---see Non-Combat Encounters for the five journey mechanics, then roll \D{}{100} in the companion volume \textbf{Journey Encounters} (\texttt{encounters.pdf}) when the adventure demands it.
