\section{How to Use This Book}

These entries are for the Monster phase in \game{} (see the core rules: Combat).
Each creature has a dice pool, Hits, Deploy tag, Threat die for Initiative, and an ordered action list resolved \textbf{top to bottom}.

Monsters do \textbf{not} spend party Energy: Medium needs a die showing at least 4; Hard Hard needs two dice showing 6.

When an adventure lists a monster by number (\#01--\#100), find that entry here.
