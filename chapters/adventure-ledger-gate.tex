\section{The Ledger Gate}

You command three companions bound by Arcane Resonance.
This adventure is a single shallow expedition through the \textbf{Guild Marches}: road, camp, Gate omen, five chambers, harvest, and return.
It teaches the expedition loop from the core rules without requiring random tables---every beat is scripted.
Have the core rules open for Combat, Basic Actions, and Campfire Counsel.
The Bestiary (\texttt{bestiary.pdf}) helps if you want full monster lore; this text lists Threat numbers and Deploy tags either way.

\subsection{What You Need}

\begin{itemize}
    \item At least fifteen six-sided dice and the party sheet from the core rules. No \dper{} is needed---every beat here is scripted.
    \item Three characters at \textbf{level 1} with attributes rolled (build your own, or use the sketches below) and their \textbf{starting kits}.
    \item Tokens or notes for zones \textbf{Z1--Z5}, Hits, Armor boxes, Debt, Resonance Stress, and party Energy.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Starting state.}
Start with the party \textbf{Energy} track full (8), \textbf{Resonance Stress 0} (track of 6), and \textbf{Debt +1} with the Mining Guild.
Each character has Hits equal to 3 plus their CON Attribute Dice and a Dice Pool of \D{2}{6}.
Checks on the road are Attribute Checks: the named character rolls their Attribute Dice for the named attribute and counts 4+ as successes---Easy needs 1 (roll at least 2 dice), Medium 2, Hard 3. Companions may add 1 die each for \energy{1}.

\subsection{The Hook}

\begin{rpgbox}{Read Aloud}
    In a March town of chalked ledgers and slag smoke, a Mining Guild factor slides a brass nail across the table.
    On it: three names---your party---and a weight in raw Aether-Glass still owed.
    They hold a co-signed relative (or the only copy of your freedom papers) until you fill a sealed Guild crate from the \textbf{Hollow Vein Gate} east of town.
    ``Bring the glass. Keep your fingers. The collector already knows your scent.''
\end{rpgbox}

Record \textbf{Debt +1}. You may instead roll a Motivation from the core book and fold this Guild demand into it; either way, the sealed weight is your goal.

\subsection{Pregen Sketches (Optional)}

Use these if you want to start immediately. Attributes are Attribute Dice; Hits follow from CON.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Warrior --- Iron Vanguard:} STR 3, DEX 2, CON 3, INT 2, WIS 3, CHA 2. Hits 6. Archetype actions Cleave, Hold the Line. Level~1 special: pick one from Iron Vanguard Level~1. \textbf{Kit:} Mace, Shield (instead of gear), Light armor.
    \item \textbf{Rogue --- Gatecutter:} STR 2, DEX 3, CON 3, INT 3, WIS 2, CHA 3. Hits 6. Skirmish, Exploit. Level~1 special: pick one from Gatecutter Level~1. \textbf{Kit:} Shortsword, Light armor, Lockpicks.
    \item \textbf{Mage --- Aetherwright:} STR 2, DEX 2, CON 2, INT 3, WIS 3, CHA 3. Hits 5. Arc Bolt, Ward. Level~1 special: pick one from Aetherwright Level~1. \textbf{Kit:} Shard-Staff, Light armor, Bandages. \textbf{Grimoire:} Glass Veil and Spark Needle (or any two Circle~I spells).
\end{itemize}

All three share Basic Actions: Move, Strike, Guard, Aid, Brace, Use Gear.

% ==========================================
\section{Act I --- Road to Hollow Vein}

Set a \kw{Journey Clock} with \textbf{4 boxes}. Resolve the ticks below in order (mark one box after each).
On the campaign map, this is four hexes from the Guild Marches Town (\textbf{E5}) to a shallow Gate; if you carry on into a campaign afterwards, pencil the Hollow Vein Gate onto a ring-D hex adjacent to your route and strike it through as sealed.
Do not roll the full non-combat table; stakes are written here (mirrors Journey Encounters \#03, \#04, \#09, \#20).

\subsection{Tick 1 --- Refugee Column}

\begin{rpgbox}{The Road}
    Families clog the March road, fleeing a Gate that woke hungry.
    Empty hands reach for crystal dust. Guild brands pink on their wrists.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Choose:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Share:} Spend 1 party Energy on food and mercy. Advance the clock as usual; gain nothing else but a clear conscience.
    \item \textbf{Refuse:} Advance the clock. Mark a \textbf{Debt rumor}: if you later meet Guild collectors in the delve, they fight with Threat as written and you cannot bargain them down.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Tick 2 --- Toll of the Free Companies}

\begin{rpgbox}{The Ridge}
    Mercenary banners stitch the ridge. Spears form a polite wall.
    ``Coin, Favor, or blood. We are not picky---only patient.''
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Choose one:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pay:} Clear any Favor you hold, or take \textbf{Debt +1} to the Companies (separate from Guild Debt). Advance clock.
    \item \textbf{Fight:} A short skirmish---Threat 3: three Ash Rats (\#02, Swarm) the Companies set loose. Win and regain 2 Energy as usual. Then advance clock.
    \item \textbf{Sneak:} One character makes a \textbf{Medium DEX check} (2 successes on their DEX dice; the others may Aid for \energy{1} each). Success: pass without payment. Failure: they notice---that character takes 1 hit \textbf{and} you mark Debt +1 to the Companies. Advance clock either way.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Tick 3 --- Collapsed Mining Sled}

\begin{rpgbox}{The Ditch}
    A sled of raw glass lies broken in a ditch, humming faintly.
    Footprints circle it like unfinished prayers.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Choose:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Salvage:} Gain 1 \textbf{Raw Aether-Glass} and mark \textbf{Resonance Stress +1}. Advance clock.
    \item \textbf{Leave it:} Advance clock. Scavengers will not blame you---yet.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Tick 4 --- Last League Markers}

\begin{rpgbox}{The Last League}
    Mileposts lean toward the Gate like teeth.
    The Hollow Vein exhales cold light over the fields.
\end{rpgbox}

One character makes an \textbf{Easy CON check} (1 success on their CON dice, rolling at least 2).
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Success:} You arrive steady. Advance clock (full). Begin Campfire.
    \item \textbf{Failure:} You arrive \kw{Exhausted}: in the next combat only, roll the party pool with \textbf{$-1$ die} (5d6 instead of 6d6 for a full party). Advance clock. Begin Campfire.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Campfire before the Gate}

Night under March sky. Energy refills to 8. Choose \textbf{two} Campfire Counsel actions from the core table (Non-Combat Encounters), each attempted by a different character. Notes for this adventure:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Heal (WIS, Medium):} Heals 2 Hits on one character---useful if the ridge went badly.
    \item \textbf{Repair (DEX, Medium):} Clears \kw{Exhausted}, or restores a tool later taken by the Omen (resolve it then; you may hold the success).
    \item \textbf{Scout (DEX, Easy):} Read Chamber 2's monster list and Deploy tags now, and you may attempt Party Ambush on that fight (Combat: Opening); you may also pick Chamber 3's choice before seeing it.
    \item \textbf{Bargain (CHA, Medium \energy{1}):} If refugees were refused, cancel the Debt rumor; otherwise clear the Companies' Debt if you took it.
    \item \textbf{Lore (INT, Easy):} The Omen here is fixed (\textit{Glass Teeth}), so take the other option: clear 1 Resonance Stress.
\end{itemize}

% ==========================================
\section{Act II --- The Ledger Gate}

\begin{rpgbox}{Threshold}
    The Hollow Vein Gate is a wound in a slag hill, rimmed with translucent growth.
    Guild chalk marks count weights that never came back.
\end{rpgbox}

\subsection{Fixed Omen --- Glass Teeth}

\begin{hazardbox}{Omen: Glass Teeth}
    The Gate rim grows teeth of living crystal.
    \textbf{Entering the delve:} each character makes an \textbf{Easy DEX check} (1 success; no Aid---everyone is busy with their own footing). Failure: \textbf{1 hit} and one tool or item is bitten away (note it); a held Campfire Repair success restores it.
    \textbf{For the whole delve:} Guard gained from Basic Actions costs a die showing 3+ instead of Easy's usual 2+---the teeth are in everything.
\end{hazardbox}

Apply this Omen for the whole delve. Then proceed to Chamber 1.

% ==========================================
\section{Act III --- Hollow Vein Delve}

Combat uses Opening (Deploy / Ambush / Initiative), Dice Pools, and Monster Phase (top to bottom) from the core rules.
\textbf{Retreat:} At any time you may flee toward Z1 and abandon the delve---lose unsealed loot (Raw glass not yet crated), keep Stress, and mark \textbf{Debt +1}.

\subsection{Chamber 1 --- Threshold Crawl (Hazard)}

\begin{hazardbox}{Static Taste}
    The air prickles. Metal sings.
    A pale cousin of Static Rain ghosts through the first corridor---enough to teach fear, not yet a full storm.
\end{hazardbox}

Any character in metal armour (the Warrior, by default) makes an \textbf{Easy DEX check} (1 success) to reach an alcove; companions may Aid.
Success: no hit. Failure: 1 hit.
Characters without metal armour are safe this chamber.
Then go to Chamber 2.

\subsection{Chamber 2 --- Tick Nest (Combat)}

\begin{rpgbox}{Nest}
    The walls glitter with fat mites and soot-rats.
    Something translucent crawls over your boots before you see it.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Threat 5} (Initiative Threat \D{1}{6}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Vein Tick \#01 --- Deploy Front --- pool 2d6, Hits 2
    \item Ash Rat \#02 --- Deploy Swarm --- pool 1d6, Hits 1
    \item Ash Rat \#02 --- Deploy Swarm --- pool 1d6, Hits 1
    \item Glass Mite \#03 --- Deploy Swarm --- pool 1d6, Hits 1
    \item Cave Urchin \#04 --- Deploy Front --- pool 2d6, Hits 2
\end{itemize}

Resolve Opening (Deploy / Ambush / Initiative). This is a Nest: if you did not Scout Chamber 2, monsters have Ambush. Fight until nest creatures are out or you retreat.
On victory, regain 2 Energy and stand any Down character with 1 Hit and a Scar (core rules: End of Fight). Go to Chamber 3.

\subsection{Chamber 3 --- Guild Cache Scratch (Choice)}

\begin{rpgbox}{Cache}
    A side niche holds a Guild strongbox scratched by panicked tools.
    Inside: a refined token stamped with Crown wax---and a wrapped Common Relic the clerks forgot to ledger.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Choose:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Steal the token:} Gain a \textbf{Refined} crystal token. Mark \textbf{Debt +1} (Guild notices later). Stress +0.
    \item \textbf{Take the relic:} Roll once on the \textbf{Common} Relic table (Equipment). Mark \textbf{Debt +1}. Binding Stress is 0 for Common.
    \item \textbf{Leave it:} Mark \textbf{Crown Favor rumor} (once this campaign, Bargain Easy when dealing with Crown agents).
\end{itemize}

Continue to Chamber 4.

\subsection{Chamber 4 --- Vein Gallery (Combat)}

\begin{rpgbox}{Gallery}
    A wider vein-gallery opens---and with it, boots and batons.
    Debt Thugs fan out; a Collector in a clerk's coat opens a ledger that already knows your names.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Threat 6} (Initiative Threat \D{1}{8} from the Collector):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Debt Thug \#17 $\times$3 --- Deploy Front --- pool 2d6, Hits 3 each
    \item Debt Collector \#56 --- Deploy Front --- pool 3d6, Hits 4, Threat d8
\end{itemize}

If you do \textbf{not} carry the \textbf{Debt rumor} from Tick 1, one character may first try a \textbf{Hard CHA check} (3 successes; Aid allowed) to talk them down: success means the Thugs leave and only the Collector fights (Threat 3); failure means combat as written and Debt +1 with the Guild.
If you still carry the Debt rumor, combat is mandatory.
When combat begins, resolve Opening (Deploy / Ambush / Initiative).
When the Collector has 1 Hit left you may let it flee instead of finishing it; note \textbf{Collector fled}: Debt +1 persists after the adventure even if you deliver the glass.

On victory (or after retreat rules), go to Chamber 5 if you still hold the delve.

\subsection{Chamber 5 --- Sealed Weight (Harvest)}

\begin{rpgbox}{The Weight}
    At the gallery's end, raw Aether-Glass beads from a cracked seam into a Guild crate already stamped with your nail-mark.
    The crystal hums. Your Resonance answers.
\end{rpgbox}

\textbf{Choose harvest:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fill the seal (required for the hook):} Take enough for the Guild crate. Mark \textbf{Resonance Stress +1}. You have the \textbf{sealed weight}.
    \item \textbf{Greed:} Also pocket 1 extra Raw for yourselves: Stress \textbf{+1} more (total +2 if you also sealed). Extra Raw is yours after delivery.
\end{itemize}

If Stress reaches 6, clear the track and roll a Corruption Mutation from the Campaign chapter.

Then extract: return through the chambers to the Gate (no extra fights unless you want them). Omen teeth do not bite on exit. On the surface, Energy refills to 8.

% ==========================================
\section{Act IV --- Surface and Aftermath}

\subsection{Delivery}

Return to the March town factor.

\begin{itemize}
    \item If you deliver the \textbf{sealed weight:} Clear the adventure's starting \textbf{Debt +1}. The relative (or papers) is released.
    \item Keep any \textbf{extra Raw}, stolen \textbf{Refined token}, or \textbf{Common Relic} if you took them.
    \item If \textbf{Collector fled} was marked: mark \textbf{Debt +1} again as a campaign hook---the ledger is not finished with you.
    \item Company Debt from the ridge toll remains until paid in a later session.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Level Up}

Each surviving character \kw{level up}s to \textbf{level 2}: choose one special action from their class's Level~2 list of five (see Classes in the core rules).

\subsection{What Comes Next}

Seeds for the next expedition:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ash Frontier} --- if ember storms interest you; shallow Clock 5.
    \item \textbf{Vein Wastes} --- if Stress already sings; mid Gate, harder Resonance.
    \item Hunt the fled Collector before World Crack rumors thicken.
\end{itemize}

\begin{rpgbox}{End Read}
    The nail is gone from the table. The Gate is not gone from your dreams.
    Somewhere under Terramyr, the Shard grows another tooth.
\end{rpgbox}
