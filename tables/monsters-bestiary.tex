\section{Minions}

Small hungers, swarms, and hired knives. Cheap Threat, ugly deaths.

\subsection*{\#01 --- Vein Tick\index{Vein Tick}}

\textbf{Appearance.} Fist-sized crystal mites with translucent carapaces veined in amber light; needle-legs click like abacus beads.

\textbf{Behavior.} They fatten on warm Aether-Glass seams, close into a fighter's zone, bite, then latch so the victim cannot flee.

\textbf{Lore.} Crystal mites fatten in warm Aether-Glass seams. Miners call their bites ``ledger kisses''---cheap wounds that open the way for worse hungers deeper in.

\begin{monsterentry}{Stats}
\textbf{Pool} 2d6\quad\textbf{Hits} 2\quad\textbf{Deploy} Front\quad\textbf{Threat} d6

\textbf{Actions (top to bottom)}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Close In} (\easy): If not sharing a zone with a character, Move toward the nearest one.
  \item \textbf{Bite} (\medium): Deal 1 hit to one character in its zone.
  \item \textbf{Latch} (\hard{} \hard{}): Deal 1 hit; that character cannot Move next round.
\end{enumerate}
\end{monsterentry}
