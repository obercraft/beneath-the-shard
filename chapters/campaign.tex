\section{Campaign}

Terramyr campaigns follow the three lore phases---\textbf{Delve and Harvest}, \textbf{Survive the Resonance}, and \textbf{Seal the Breach}---as a repeating expedition loop between the surface and the Star-Grave.
First-time players should run the companion adventure \textbf{The Ledger Gate} (\texttt{adventure.pdf}) once before improvising full campaigns.

\subsection{Expedition Loop}

Run each expedition in this order:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Setup (once):} Roll a Party Motivation (Campaign Setup table). Build three characters (archetype $\rightarrow$ class $\rightarrow$ level~1 special action). Note starting Debt or Favor implied by the Motivation. Choose a home Town on the Map of Terramyr (Guild Marches, \textbf{E5}, unless the Motivation says otherwise).
    \item \textbf{Outfit:} In a Town, set \kw{Supply} to 6 and Energy to 8. Pay any Debt you intend to pay.
    \item \textbf{Travel:} Move one hex at a time (Hexploration below). Each hex entered costs 1 Supply and triggers one beat from \textbf{Journey Encounters} (\texttt{encounters.pdf}).
    \item \textbf{Discover:} The first time you enter a hex, roll its Feature. Pencil it onto the map. Gates stay where you find them.
    \item \textbf{Camp:} Anywhere, for 1 Supply: Energy refills and you take two Campfire Counsel actions. Camp at least once before a Gate.
    \item \textbf{Omen:} At the Gate, roll a Shard-Gate Omen. Apply its twist for the whole delve.
    \item \textbf{Delve:} Explore 3--7 chambers (Chamber Themes below). A chamber may be a hazard, a choice, or a combat encounter built with the Threat Budget for the Gate's ring.
    \item \textbf{Harvest:} Taking Aether-Glass raises Resonance Stress. Selling or spending crystals feeds Debt, Favor, and gear.
    \item \textbf{Return:} Walk back hex by hex to a Town to sell, level up, and refit. A sealed Gate is struck through on the map.
    \item \textbf{Deepen:} Shallow rings pay for gear and levels; ring \textbf{B} Gates open the way to the Star-Grave.
    \item \textbf{Finale:} Enter \textbf{A0}. Face Heart-tier horrors. Stress and Debt come due. Seal or destroy the Heart---or fail.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Hexploration}

The Map of Terramyr (next page) has five rings. Ring \textbf{E} is the surface rim with six Towns; \textbf{D} and \textbf{E} hold shallow Gates, \textbf{C} mid Gates, \textbf{B} deep Gates, and \textbf{A0} is the Star-Grave itself. Wedges are the Surface Regions. Movement is always to an adjacent hex.

\paragraph{Entering a hex.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Mark \textbf{Supply $-1$}. Encounters that say ``clock $+1$'' also mean Supply $-1$; ``clock $-1$'' restores one.
    \item If the hex is \textbf{unexplored}, roll \D{1}{6} on the Hex Feature table for its ring and write the letter on the map.
    \item Roll \D{}{100} on Journey Encounters and resolve it. In an \textbf{explored} hex, roll \D{1}{6} first and only draw an encounter on a 1--2.
    \item Resolve the Feature (visit the camp, bargain at the post, delve the Gate, or move on).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Supply.}
Supply is the expedition's Journey Clock. At \textbf{0}, every further hex costs one of the clock prices (Late, Lost, or Exhausted---see Non-Combat Encounters) \textbf{and} $+1$ Resonance Stress. Supply refills to 6 in any Town and by 2 at a Camp feature.

\paragraph{Gates.}
A discovered Gate stays on the map. Delving it needs an Omen and a Campfire first. Once its delve is completed and the party returns to the surface, the Gate is \textbf{sealed}: strike it through; it yields nothing more. If you retreat or are Broken, it stays open with its Omen already rolled.

\paragraph{The Star-Grave.}
\textbf{A0} cannot be entered until the party has sealed \textbf{two ring-B Gates}---or until the World Crack Clock fills, when A0 opens from any adjacent hex whether you are ready or not. Entering A0 begins the finale (Heart Threat Budget, mandatory Omen).

\paragraph{Counsel on the road.}
Campfire \textbf{Scout} may instead reveal the Feature of one adjacent hex without entering it. Campfire \textbf{Lore} may instead roll and record the Omen of a known Gate before you travel there.

\subsection{Campaign Length}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Short campaign:} 6 Gates (2 shallow, 2 mid, 2 deep), then the Prismatic Heart. Two sealed ring-B Gates open \textbf{A0}.
    \item \textbf{Long campaign:} 10 Gates across more wedges, allowing characters to approach level~10 before the Heart.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Victory:} Stabilize or destroy the Prismatic Heart.\\
\textbf{Failure:} Resonance Stress maxes into thralldom, Debt foreclosure ends the party, or the World Crack Clock fills and the finale begins whether you are ready or not.

\subsection{Leveling}

When you \textbf{clear a Gate tier} (complete the delve and return, or secure the Gate as the adventure requires), each surviving character \kw{level up}s by one level: choose one special action from that class level's list of five, and add $+10$ to one attribute (maximum 80; see Character Creation: Attribute Rank and Dice). On reaching \textbf{level 3, 6, and 9} the character's combat Dice Pool grows by one die (\D{3}{6}, \D{4}{6}, \D{5}{6}); a Mage's spell limit grows with level as well (Spells).

Optional: also level when a Motivation beat resolves in a major way (your judgment---once per Gate at most). A character lost to Thrall Crack or left behind in a Broken party may be replaced: build a new companion at the party's lowest level and mark \textbf{Debt +1} with the faction that buried, bought, or freed them.

\subsection{Aether-Glass Economy}

Crystals come in three grades:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Raw:} Harvested in veins. Recharge 1 party Energy once, then gone. Mark $+1$ Resonance Stress when used or carried out of a Gate.
    \item \textbf{Refined:} Buy gear and Grimoire Pages, sell for Favor, bribe (clear 1 Debt pressure), or power a gear mod for one expedition. $+1$ Stress when spent as fuel (not when spent as shop price).
    \item \textbf{Prismatic:} Rare deep loot. Bind a Prismatic Relic, fuel a Heart-key, or hold for the finale. $+2$ Stress when bound to a character.
\end{itemize}

You may dump crystals before exiting to reduce Stress at the cost of wealth and Motivation progress.

\subsection{World Crack Clock}

Start a campaign clock with \textbf{8 boxes}. Advance it when:

\begin{itemize}
    \item You fail or abandon a \textbf{deep} Gate ( $+1$ ).
    \item You ignore a Heart Whisper chamber or a finale Omen ( $+1$ ).
    \item A horror escapes the delve onto the surface ( $+1$ ).
    \item Motivation demands delay while the Shard grows (adventure triggers).
\end{itemize}

When the clock \textbf{fills}, begin the Prismatic Heart finale immediately---\textbf{A0} opens from any adjacent hex, Supply cannot be refilled, Omens are mandatory, and Threat Budgets use deep/Heart values. Clearing shallow Gates does not reduce the clock; only sealing/destroying the Heart ends it.
