\section{Spells}

Only \textbf{Mages} keep a \kw{Grimoire}. Arc Bolt and Ward remain archetype actions; spells are extra patterns learned from pages, stalls, and tombs.

\subsection{Knowing Spells}

\begin{itemize}
    \item A Mage knows up to $\mathbf{2 + \lfloor \textrm{level}/2 \rfloor}$ spells (level 1: 2; level 4: 4; level 10: 7).
    \item At character creation, choose \textbf{two Circle~I} spells. Write them on the party sheet.
    \item To learn another spell, obtain a \textbf{Grimoire Page} of that Circle and spend a Camp or Town rest copying it. If at your limit, forget one known spell to make room.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Finding pages.}
Shrine defy, Glass Market, and Relic Tombs can yield pages (Campaign: Chamber Themes). Towns sell Circle~I pages for \textbf{2 Refined} or \textbf{1 Cult Favor}. Higher Circles are delve loot only.

\subsection{Casting}

Casting a spell is an action in the Party phase: assign dice from \textbf{this Mage only} to meet the printed difficulty, pay Energy and any Resonance Stress marked on the spell, then resolve the effect. Spells marked \defensive{} are defensive actions (Combat: Defense).

\paragraph{Overcast.}
When you cast, you may mark \textbf{$+1$ Resonance Stress} to apply the spell's \textbf{Overcast} boost. You cannot Overcast if the Stress track is already full (resolve a mutation first if it fills from other sources).

\paragraph{Shard-Staff.}
A Mage wielding a Shard-Staff (Equipment) may, once per round, raise one die face on a spell by $+1$ (max 6) before checking difficulty.
