\section{Equipment}

Steel, leather, and Aether-Glass keep a party alive between Gates. Every character has four kinds of slot; what fills them is written on the party sheet.

\subsection{Slots}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Weapon (1):} one weapon. A \textbf{2h} weapon also fills the off-hand slot.
    \item \textbf{Off-hand (1):} shield, buckler, parrying knife, or empty (required empty for 2h weapons).
    \item \textbf{Armor (1):} none, Light, Medium, or Heavy. Armor boxes absorb hits after Guard (Combat: Defense).
    \item \textbf{Gear (4):} tools, consumables, and tokens. Each item takes one slot unless the text says otherwise.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Starting kit.}
As Character Creation: one weapon of your choice, Light armor, and one gear item. One character may take a Shield instead of that gear item. Fists are free: Strike with no weapon deals 1 hit and nothing more.

\subsection{Catalog}

Prices are in \textbf{Refined} crystals. Towns sell the full list; Faction Posts on the map sell \textbf{gear only}.

\subsection{Shops and Spoils}

\paragraph{Buying.}
Pay the listed Refined price in a Town (or gear-only at a Faction Post). You may sell mundane gear back for half price (round down, minimum 0). Relics are never sold in ordinary shops.

\paragraph{Spoils.}
When you \textbf{seal a Gate} and Return to a Town, the party gains Refined by the Gate's ring, plus any Raw harvested in veins:

Split loot as you like. Unsealed loot left behind on Retreat or Broken is lost (Campaign / Combat).

\subsection{Relics}

\kw{Relic}s are Shard-touched treasures. They are not shop goods.

\begin{itemize}
    \item A character may have \textbf{one Relic bound} and \textbf{one Relic carried} (unbound, no benefit).
    \item \textbf{Binding} costs Resonance Stress by rarity (below). Unbinding in a Town clears the benefit but not Stress already marked.
    \item Relic effects that grant actions use your Dice Pool and Energy like any other action.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Finding relics.}
When a chamber or encounter grants a Relic, roll on the table for the Gate's ring (or the rarity named). On higher tables, high d20 results can step up in rarity.
