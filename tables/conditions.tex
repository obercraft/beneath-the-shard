\kw{Guard} & When it absorbs a hit, or at the end of the fight & Ignore the first hit you would take. A character or monster never holds more than one Guard unless an action says ``Guard twice''. \\
\kw{Bound} & End of the next round, or as the action states & Cannot \kw{Move} or be Moved. Other actions are unaffected. \\
\kw{Marked} & When the mark is used, or at the end of the fight & The next hit dealt to this figure by the marking side deals $+1$ hit. Several Marks on one figure each add $+1$ to that same hit. \\
\kw{Burning} & When the figure gains Guard, or at the end of the fight & Takes 1 hit at the start of each round. \\
\kw{Down} & When the fight is won (stand with 1 Hit and a Scar) or the party is Broken & 0 Hits. Contributes no dice, acts not at all, and cannot be targeted while an ally is standing. \\
\kw{Scarred} & Never & Permanent. Maximum Hits reduced by 1 per Scar, never below 3. \\
\kw{Exhausted} & After the party's first fight of the delve, or by Campfire Repair & The party rolls $-1$ die in its next fight. \\
\kw{Lost} & When the next encounter is resolved & Resolve one extra encounter before you reach the Gate. \\
