\section{The Shard of Terramyr}

\subsection{The Cataclysm}
In the frozen silence of the deep cosmos, a nameless, distant planet burned with the volatile brilliance of pure magic. There, an ancient cabal of archmages pushed the boundaries of the arcane too far. Seeking to bind the very fabric of reality to their will, their final, catastrophic experiment tore the core of their world apart. In a single, silent heartbeat, the planet fractured into a billion pieces, scattering raw, condensed magic across the stars.

Most of the debris dissolved into cosmic dust. But one colossal fragment—a mountain-sized asteroid pulsing with untamed, world-shattering arcane energy—hurtled through the void for eons, until it caught the gravity of a young, medieval world named \textbf{Terramyr}.

\begin{rpgbox}{Subterranean Hazard: Static Rain}
    Inside the Shard-Dungeons, fields of raw energy can trigger \textit{Static Rain}. 
    Every explorer wearing chainmail or plate armor takes 1d4 lightning damage 
    per turn unless they find immediate stone shelter.
\end{rpgbox}

\subsection{The Impact}
The day the sky tore open is known in the chronicles as \textit{The Night of the Falling Sun}. The colossus struck the earth with the wrath of gods. Cities turned to ash, oceans boiled, and the ground wept fire. Yet, the world did not shatter. Instead, the asteroid buried itself miles beneath the crust, driven deep into the belly of the world. 

The mortal survivors named this cosmic invader \textbf{The Shard}.

\subsection{The Changing World}
The Shard did not remain dormant. Driven by the alien magic within, it began to grow. Like a crystalline cancer, it spread its glowing, jagged veins throughout the underworld. As the crystals pushed through rock and soil, they carved out impossible spaces—shattering old cavern systems and creating vast, glowing networks of subterranean vaults, deep chasms, and shifting dungeons. 

\subsection{The Dawn of Sorcery}
Above ground, humanity and the other medieval kingdoms slowly rebuilt from the ruins. It didn’t take long for ambitious souls to discover that fragments of The Shard could be harvested. 

These mined crystal shards are now the world’s most precious and dangerous commodity. Blacksmiths forge them into weapons that strike with the force of lightning; scholars grind them into dust to brew volatile potions; and a new breed of mages uses them as catalysts to cast spells never before seen on this world.

\section{Terramyr Today \& The Quest Ahead}

\subsection{A Kingdom of Iron, A Heart of Glass}
A century has passed since \textit{The Night of the Falling Sun}, and Terramyr is a world caught between two eras. On the surface, the medieval kingdoms still cling to their traditions. Knights in heavy steel guard stone keeps, and peasants toil in the shadow of ancient forests. But beneath this familiar facade, the world is radically transforming. 

Society has adapted out of sheer desperation. Terramyr is now driven by a gold rush for the arcane. The Crown, the powerful Mining Guilds, and secretive cults all vie for control over the dungeon entrances—known as \textbf{Shard-Gates}. Steel is no longer enough; to survive in this new Terramyr, one must wield the glass.

\subsection{The Calling of the Three}
In \textit{Shardbound}, you do not play a solitary chosen hero. The depth of the Star-Grave is too unforgiving for a lone wanderer. Instead, you command a \textbf{bound party of three companions}—a small fellowship of survivors, mercenaries, or scholars thrust into the dark together. 

Your party is bound by a shared destiny, a desperate need for wealth, or a dark secret. Unlike typical armies, your small group possesses a rare trait: a high \textbf{Arcane Resonance}, allowing you to harvest and handle the raw crystals of The Shard without instantly losing your sanity or transforming into a monster.

\subsection{Your Primary Objective}
As the guide of this party, your overarching campaign objective is divided into three distinct phases:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Delve and Harvest:} You must locate Shard-Gates across the map of Terramyr and venture into the ever-shifting subterranean dungeons to mine raw \textit{Aether-Glass}.
    \item \textbf{Survive the Resonance:} You must balance the immense power the crystals grant your weapons with the creeping corruption that threatens to twist your party into mindless thralls.
    \item \textbf{Seal the Breach:} You must find the \textbf{Prismatic Heart} at the absolute bottom of the Star-Grave and stabilize—or destroy—it before the Shard shatters Terramyr.
\end{itemize}
