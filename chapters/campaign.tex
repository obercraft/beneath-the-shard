\section{Campaign}

Terramyr campaigns follow the three lore phases---\textbf{Delve and Harvest}, \textbf{Survive the Resonance}, and \textbf{Seal the Breach}---as a repeating expedition loop between the surface and the Star-Grave.
First-time players should run the companion adventure \textbf{The Ledger Gate} (\texttt{adventure.pdf}) once before improvising full campaigns.

\subsection{Expedition Loop}

Run each expedition in this order:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Setup (once):} Roll a Party Motivation (Campaign Setup table). Build three characters (archetype $\rightarrow$ class $\rightarrow$ level~1 special action). Note starting Debt or Favor implied by the Motivation.
    \item \textbf{Choose a Gate:} Pick or roll a Surface Region (below). Set a \kw{Journey Clock} (usually 4--6 boxes).
    \item \textbf{Travel:} On each clock tick, resolve a non-combat encounter from the companion volume \textbf{Journey Encounters} (\texttt{encounters.pdf})---roll \D{}{100} or pick by mechanic band. Update Resonance Stress and Debt/Favor as directed.
    \item \textbf{Camp:} Before the Gate, use Campfire Counsel---assign party dice to Heal, Repair, Scout, Bargain, and Lore.
    \item \textbf{Omen:} At the Gate, roll or choose a Shard-Gate Omen. Apply its twist for the whole delve.
    \item \textbf{Delve:} Explore 3--7 chambers (Chamber Themes below). A chamber may be a hazard, a choice, or a combat encounter built with the Threat Budget.
    \item \textbf{Harvest:} Taking Aether-Glass raises Resonance Stress. Selling or spending crystals feeds Debt, Favor, and gear.
    \item \textbf{Return or deepen:} Clear shallow Gates for gear and levels; push mid and deep Gates toward the Prismatic Heart.
    \item \textbf{Finale:} Face Heart-tier horrors. Stress and Debt come due. Seal or destroy the Heart---or fail.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Campaign Length}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Short campaign:} 6 Gates (2 shallow, 2 mid, 2 deep), then the Prismatic Heart.
    \item \textbf{Long campaign:} 10 Gates across more regions, allowing characters to approach level~10 before the Heart.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Victory:} Stabilize or destroy the Prismatic Heart.\\
\textbf{Failure:} Resonance Stress maxes into thralldom, Debt foreclosure ends the party, or the World Crack Clock fills and the finale begins whether you are ready or not.

\subsection{Leveling}

When you \textbf{clear a Gate tier} (complete the delve and return, or secure the Gate as the adventure requires), each surviving character \kw{level up}s by one level: choose one special action from that class level's list of five.

Optional: also level when a Motivation beat resolves in a major way (GM/solo judgment). Characters who fall may be replaced; see Legacy under future Bond rules---for now, build a new companion at the party's lowest level.

\subsection{Aether-Glass Economy}

Crystals come in three grades:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Raw:} Harvested in veins. Recharge 1 party Energy once, then gone. Mark $+1$ Resonance Stress when used or carried out of a Gate.
    \item \textbf{Refined:} Sold for Favor, used to bribe (clear 1 Debt pressure), or to power a gear mod for one expedition. $+1$ Stress when spent.
    \item \textbf{Prismatic:} Rare deep loot. Permanent relic fuel or Heart-key material. $+2$ Stress when bound to a character.
\end{itemize}

You may dump crystals before exiting to reduce Stress at the cost of wealth and Motivation progress.

\subsection{World Crack Clock}

Start a campaign clock with \textbf{8 boxes}. Advance it when:

\begin{itemize}
    \item You fail or abandon a \textbf{deep} Gate ( $+1$ ).
    \item You ignore a Heart Whisper chamber or a finale Omen ( $+1$ ).
    \item A horror escapes the delve onto the surface ( $+1$ ).
    \item Motivation demands delay while the Shard grows (adventure triggers).
\end{itemize}

When the clock \textbf{fills}, begin the Prismatic Heart finale immediately---travel clocks compress to 2, Omens are mandatory, and Threat Budgets use deep/Heart values. Clearing shallow Gates does not reduce the clock; only sealing/destroying the Heart ends it.
